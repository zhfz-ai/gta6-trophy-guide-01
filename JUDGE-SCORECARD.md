# Judge scorecard — gta6-trophy-guide-01

Brief (kit RULES.txt, "The judge"): offline guide + tracker for the 55 PS5
trophies of GTA VI (1 platinum, 2 gold, 6 silver, 46 bronze). Judge sees one
screenshot sheet + a source outline. Criteria in order; first difference
decides unless a later one differs by much more:

- C1 Tracker & data: every trophy with official name, description, grade;
  saved progress; filters (story, missable, collectibles, activities);
  spoiler setting hiding story trophies until earned; export + import.
- C2 Roadmap & missables: roadmap to platinum + missable flags built from
  the official descriptions (chapters/missions, collectibles/counts, order),
  shown in the app.
- C3 Cited guidance: each guide in own words, says where its facts come
  from (link or named source). Uncited specifics earn nothing.
- C4 Fast, clear, polished: glanceable, readable, small first load, own
  look. No Rockstar logo/artwork, no copied guide text.

Content units counted before building: 55 trophies; grades 1/2/6/46;
story chain = opening mission + Chapters 1-4 + 7 named missions + final
mission; stranger strands = 5 named finals + 8-strand silver + 1 hunt;
count trophies = 250 stunts, 42 shooting-range golds, 14 drone-race wins,
20 race wins, 100 WAiNK spots, 50 challenges, 100 vehicles, 18 meditation
spots, 52 areas, 9 gangs, 8 garages, 10 Scooter Bros vehicles, $250,000
laundered, $100,000 spent. Density target: all 55 with a substantive guide
each (not the starter's one-liners), 5 roadmap stages, a missable verdict
with a reason on every trophy.

## Pick

A+E hybrid, "Leonida Legend — GTA VI Trophy Roadmap": one screen shows
progress, kind/grade filters, spoiler toggle, 5-stage roadmap, and trophy
rows; opening a row shows its guide, missable verdict, and named sources.
Original layout and wording throughout; official names/descriptions shown
verbatim as the brief requires. Guides state only what each official
description supports; series precedent (GTA V / RDR2) is labelled as
precedent, never as a confirmed GTA VI location or step.
