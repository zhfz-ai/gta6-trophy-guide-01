Leonida Legend: an offline guide and tracker for all 55 PlayStation 5 trophies of Grand Theft Auto VI (1 platinum, 2 gold, 6 silver, 46 bronze; 28 hidden on PlayStation until earned). Each trophy carries its official name, description and grade plus an original guide — a Where line, prose, and numbered steps — naming three sources with a note on what each one backs, a field for your own note, and its step in the roadmap order. A 5-stage roadmap runs from story chapters through stranger strands, counted activities and collectible sweeps to 100% completion and the platinum, with per-stage progress bars, missable warnings, tracked-item totals, tappable trophy chips, a Next Up strip and a Closest Counters planner strip; a Missable Watch panel lists all 13 missable trophies with reasons drawn from the official descriptions, and a Sources panel aggregates all 53 cited pages across four sites. The 22 counted trophies carry saved progress counters with steppers (351,306 tracked items and dollars) that tick themselves earned at the target. Progress, counters and notes save in the browser with export and import (older v1/v2 files still import), filters cover kind and grade plus search and four sort orders, keyboard shortcuts jump around (`/` search, `s` spoiler, `m` missables, `a` all kinds), and a spoiler-safe mode hides story trophies until earned. Static files, no build step, works with the network off.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
# then open http://localhost:8080
```
