Leonida Legend: an offline guide and tracker for all 55 PlayStation 5 trophies of Grand Theft Auto VI (1 platinum, 2 gold, 6 silver, 46 bronze; 28 hidden on PlayStation until earned). Every trophy shows its official name, description and grade next to an original guide — a Where line, an effort line (estimated time and difficulty derived from the official count), prose, and numbered steps — with three named sources and a note on what each one backs, a field for your own note, and its step in the roadmap order. The checklist leads the page, with progress per grade and per kind plus the items and estimated time still to go; the 5-stage roadmap runs from story chapters through stranger strands, counted activities and collectible sweeps to 100% completion and the platinum, carrying per-stage progress bars, missable warnings, tracked-item totals, stage time estimates, tappable trophy chips, a Next Up strip and a Closest Counters planner strip. A Platinum Planner table totals what remains in each stage — trophies, tracked items, estimated time, missable risk — and picks Quick Wins for a short session; the Missable Watch panel lists all 13 missable trophies with reasons drawn from the official descriptions, and a Sources panel aggregates all 53 cited pages across four sites. The 22 counted trophies carry saved progress counters with steppers (351,306 tracked items and dollars) that tick themselves earned at the target. Progress, counters and notes save in the browser with export and import (older v1/v2 files still import); filters cover kind and grade, search reads names, descriptions and guide text, five sort orders include quickest-first, guides expand and collapse together, keyboard shortcuts jump around (`/` search, `s` spoiler, `m` missables, `a` all kinds, `e` expand all), and a spoiler-safe mode hides story trophies until earned. Static files, no build step, works with the network off.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
# then open http://localhost:8080
```
